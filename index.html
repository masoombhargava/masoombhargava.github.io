About

The questions I work on, and the path that led here.

My research focuses on mathematical and data-informed modeling to investigate complex dynamics in biological systems. I develop models grounded in nonlinear dynamics and use bifurcation theory to analyse tipping points and critical transitions in nonlinear, stochastic, and multi-scale living systems.

I am especially interested in integrating experimental and observational data to inform model structure and parameterization. My work spans spatiotemporal dynamics in biological and ecological systems, infectious-disease modeling, and tumour growth, with an emphasis on how a handful of key parameters drive the emergence of complex biological patterns.

The larger aim is to uncover the mechanisms behind critical transitions across scales - from ecological networks to physiological processes. By pairing rigorous modeling with data-driven methods, I look for the core features, the biological switches and oscillators, that govern a system's resilience and progression, so that models can offer genuine predictive insight rather than description alone.

Research

Six threads that run through the work. Open one to see how it connects.

∿

Nonlinear dynamics & bifurcation theory

Locating the parameter thresholds where a system's behaviour changes qualitatively - Hopf and saddle-node bifurcations, limit cycles, chaos, and the early-warning signals that precede a critical transition.

stability · tipping points · chaos · delays

❋

Pattern formation & spatiotemporal dynamics

How macroscopic structure arises in space and time - reaction–diffusion systems, Turing instabilities, and how seasonal forcing and movement reshape the patterns that form.

Turing patterns · reaction–diffusion · prey-taxis

✚

Mathematical biology

Mechanistic ODE/PDE models of biological processes - tumour–immune interactions and chemo-immunotherapy, and the spread of infectious disease.

tumour–immune · chemo-immunotherapy · disease spread

❧

Ecological modeling & trade-off dynamics

Prey–predator systems where survival carries a cost: refuge, fear effects, group defence, and harvesting can push a community between stability and collapse.

prey–predator · refuge & fear · group defence

⬡

Dynamics on networks

Moving models onto networks - including multiplex networks - to ask how topology governs pattern formation and the spread of activity across coupled populations.

ecological networks · multiplex · network patterns

≋

Stochastic & multi-scale systems

Bringing noise into deterministic models, to test how environmental stochasticity and periodic forcing change stability, persistence, and the onset of chaos.

environmental noise · random fluctuations · forcing
